Series questions present a sequence of numbers or letters following a hidden pattern, such as adding a constant, multiplying by a constant, or squares, and ask you to find the next term.

Always check the difference or ratio between consecutive terms first. If that's not constant, check for squares, cubes, or an alternating pattern where odd and even positions follow different rules.

A common mistake is giving up after checking only simple addition -- many series need a second or third hypothesis before the pattern becomes clear.
