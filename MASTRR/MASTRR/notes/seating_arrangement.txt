Seating arrangement puzzles place people around a table (linear or circular) based on given clues, and ask you to determine the final order or specific positions.

For circular arrangements, remember that left and right depend on whether people are facing the center or facing outward -- this changes which direction is clockwise from each person's point of view.

A common mistake is not fixing one person's position as a reference point first -- always fix one seat mentally and build the rest of the arrangement relative to it.
