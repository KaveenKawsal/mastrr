Blood relation questions describe family connections using statements like "A is the son of B" and ask you to determine the relationship between two people. The key skill is drawing a simple family tree diagram as you read each clue.

A common mistake is assuming gender from context without it being explicitly stated -- for example, "A's child" could be a son or daughter unless specified, and wrongly assuming gender leads to wrong answers.

Always label each person on your diagram with their given gender and generation level before working out the final relationship.
