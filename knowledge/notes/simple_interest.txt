Simple Interest (SI) is the interest calculated only on the original principal amount, not on any interest that has accumulated. It depends on three quantities: Principal (P), Rate of interest per annum (R), and Time in years (T).

The formula is SI = (P x R x T) / 100. For example, the simple interest on a principal of 1000 at 10% per annum for 3 years is (1000x10x3)/100 = 300. The total amount (A) repaid is A = P + SI.

A common mistake is forgetting that SI grows linearly -- the interest earned each year is identical, unlike compound interest where it grows each year. Also, always check the rate is stated "per annum" before applying it directly to a time period given in months (convert months to years first: divide by 12).
