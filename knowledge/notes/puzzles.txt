Puzzle questions combine multiple types of clues at once (seating, blood relations, ranking, or attributes) into a single multi-step problem. The key strategy is to process the clue that gives the MOST certain information first, rather than reading strictly top to bottom.

A common mistake is trying to hold the entire puzzle in your head instead of drawing a table or grid to track what's confirmed, possible, and ruled out.

Once one position or value is pinned down exactly, use it immediately to eliminate options in the remaining clues, rather than solving all clues independently.
